# Vesper64 Surface Brief

- **Mode:** Operate (interactive multiplayer chess arena)
- **Audience:** Site Owner, invited friends (guests or optional accounts), and Owner-granted troll players
- **Primary Task:** Create or join a room via link/code, play crisp real-time chess across devices without forced signup, and (for authorized accounts only) trigger a concealed troll operator panel

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** A precision foundry-specimen tournament chess studio on dark slate where oversized variable-weight countdown clocks and hairline-ruled coordinates anchor the arena, while authorized accounts secretly unlock an invisible troll operator drawer. Refuses cluttered chess-portal sidebars, noisy card chrome, and obvious gag aesthetics.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Deep slate ground `#101216`, layered panel surface `#151921`, 1px structural dividers `#262c38`, primary alabaster ink `#f3f4f6`, muted slate ink `#94a3b8`, crisp alabaster-and-slate chessboard `#e5e7ec` / `#343c4a`, foundry crimson `#e11d48` reserved for active turn clock, primary CTA, and check states. Self-hosted variable `Figtree` (`300–900`) and `JetBrains Mono` (`400–700`) with live `font-variation-settings` weight shift on active clocks and low-time urgency.
- **STORY:** A friend opens a shared room link, immediately recognizes a serious competitive chessboard, clicks Play as Guest or optionally signs in, and plays smoothly across devices. The Owner (or a user with an active permanent/temporary grant) unlocks the hidden operator drawer via secret hotkey or hidden trigger to bend rules, manipulate clocks, trigger mind games, or consult private engine hints without leaving a trace on normal accounts.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** A hairline-ruled 52px top bar (`Vesper64` mark, `Arena` / `Create Room` / `Play Bot` navigation, compact room invite chip with copy action, optional `Account` button) over a full-height asymmetric split grid: left 380px telemetry column with oversized variable-weight player clocks (`05:00.0`, shifting from `500` to `800` weight on active turn/low time), captured material balance, and live move ledger; right main stage centering the crisp 8x8 SVG-piece chessboard and match action bar (`Flip`, `Offer Draw`, `Resign`).
